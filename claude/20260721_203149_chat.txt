**On the 16mA vs 130mA question** — I don't think it's a sun/battery blend, and here's why: V50 is fed by two sources that are diode-OR'd, not blended:
- **D2** passes panel power straight through from V50_I when the panel is strong enough.
- **U5 (boost)** takes VBAT in and pushes it up to a fixed regulated output on V50, only when **MAX809L** (U6) detects V50_I has sagged below its ~4.63V threshold — and once boost is driving V50 to its regulated level, that reverse-biases D2, so the panel path drops out entirely.

It's a hard switch between "panel powers everything" and "battery powers everything," not a proportional mix.
