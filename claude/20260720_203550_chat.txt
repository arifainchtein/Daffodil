## What changed on the new Daffodil PCB vs. the board the firmware currently targets (Build 7)

I compared the KiCad netlists (current `HardwareDesign/Daffodil` vs. the `Daffodil_Build7_27062026.zip` archive that matches the firmware's own "Build 7" comments), and cross-checked pin routing against `HardwareDesign/Wally`'s header to confirm actual GPIO identities rather than trusting net label text.

**1. The one change that actually matters for firmware — the ADS1115 sense point moved (`R1`: was on `V50`, now on `V50_I`)**
`Daffodil.ino:1169-1174` and `:1705-1707` read ADS1115 channel 3 into `digitalStablesData.v50Voltage`, under the explicit assumption at `Daffodil.ino:1182-1183`:
```

// Battery voltage must NOT be used here — it is a separate 3.2V rail
```
On the new board that assumption is false: `R1` now samples `V50_I` (the raw, pre-diode solar/USB input) instead of the regulated `V50` rail. This channel will now swing from ~0V at night to ~5V+ in full sun instead of sitting flat at ~5V. This is the same net the new `MAX809L` (U6) watches for its 4.63V boost-enable threshold — the hardware team moved the sense point specifically to expose real solar presence.
**Action:** update that comment, and decide whether to use this now-meaningful reading in firmware (real solar-detection signal, previously only inferred from the config switch / `usingSolarPower`). Also check the web app/CSV export doesn't assume `v50Voltage` ≈ constant 5V anywhere.

**2. Boost converter + solar-detect supervisor redesign (Build 10, already in your memory) — no firmware change needed**

**5. Everything digital is unchanged and verified:** I2C addresses (INA219 @0x41, ADS1115, etc.), `GPIO_18`/`GPIO_33` (shared ultrasonic/flow pins), config switch, TPL5010. This is already fully handled as a permanent, not just transient, case. Tracing it through:

- `foundBH1750` (`Daffodil.ino:257`) is a plain global, **not** `RTC_DATA_ATTR` — so it resets to `false` on every wake and gets re-derived purely from that boot's I2C scan (`Daffodil.ino` I2C scan, addr `0x23`). A unit that simply never has a BH1750 populated will have `foundBH1750 == false` on every single cycle, forever — not an intermittent glitch.
- Cloudy decision (`luxSaysCloudy = foundBH1750 && ...`): permanently `false` on such a unit → decision is `v50iSaysCloudy || forecastSaysCloudy` only.
- Sleep extension (`_lowLightForSleep = lux >= 0 && ...`): `lux` is set to `-99` whenever `!foundBH1750` (`Daffodil.ino:1741`), so `-99 >= 0` is permanently `false` → falls through to `_lowV50IForSleep` only.

So on a lux-less unit, V50_I (plus forecast, when weather data is fresh) becomes the sole cloudy signal, with lux contributing nothing rather than silently defaulting to "not cloudy" or some stale/zero value. Both boolean expressions are structured as independent OR terms, not a lux-primary/V50-fallback chain — there's no ordering dependency between them.
