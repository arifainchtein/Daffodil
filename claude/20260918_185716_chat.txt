I checked the WiFi startup path (`DaffodilWifiManager::start()` → `scanNetworks()` → `connectSTA()`/`connectAP()` in the `DigitalStablesEsp32Lib` library). A few things stood out:

- `scanNetworks()` does an async `WiFi.scanNetworks(true)` then polls `scanComplete()` in a loop — bounded to 60 × 500ms (~30s), not infinite.
- `connectSTA()` calls `WiFi.begin()` then polls with a 20s timeout, also bounded.
- Neither loop should hang forever on its own — so if it's *truly* stuck (not just slow), the likely causes are: a brownout/reset loop from the bench power supply not sustaining the WiFi radio's current spikes (common on USB-serial bench power), or it's actually progressing slowly through the ~30s scan timeout and just looks frozen.

I also noticed you have an uncommitted change in `setup()` (qfactor loading from `secretManager.getQFactor1()/getQFactor2()`) that runs *before* the WiFi start further down — worth ruling out too, though it doesn't touch WiFi/NVS-adjacent calls in an obviously blocking way.

Can you paste the last several lines of serial output right before/at the hang? Specifically I want to see:
1. Is it stuck exactly after `"startscan poiomnt 1"` / `"2"`, or after `"in connectSTA ssid="`, or somewhere else?
2. Is the board silently frozen, or is it printing boot banners again (i.e. actually resetting in a loop)?

That'll tell me whether this is a bounded-timeout-just-looks-stuck situation, a brownout reset loop, or an actual infinite loop I'm not seeing yet.
